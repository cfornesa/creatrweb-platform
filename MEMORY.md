2026-06-03 · PIECE EMBED LAZY LOADING · Removed `frame.removeAttribute` from the `mount()` function in `PostContent.tsx`. Root cause: `normalizePieceEmbedFrame` sets `loading="lazy"` on the iframe before it is stored in the `<template>`. When the outer IntersectionObserver (250px rootMargin) fires and `mount()` clones the iframe into the live DOM, the browser re-evaluates `loading="lazy"` against its own threshold (~200px on fast connections). If the piece is at 200–250px from the viewport, the outer observer mounts the iframe but the browser defers fetching it — leaving the `bg-muted` mount div background visible with a transparent unloaded iframe on top. Fix: `frame.removeAttribute("loading")` just before `media.appendChild(frame)`. Lazy loading is still fully provided by the `<template>` + IntersectionObserver pattern — iframes are never in the DOM until the observer fires.
    [Fixed 2026-06-03; PostContent.tsx enhanceLazyIframes mount(); typecheck clean.]
